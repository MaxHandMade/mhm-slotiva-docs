Setup wizard

The wizard opens right after you activate Slotiva. It takes about three minutes and every answer can be changed later. You can leave at any time with Finish later; your progress is saved step by step.

Step 1 — Service​

Choose what you sell: Consulting, Lesson / training, Appointment / session or Another service. Slotiva uses your choice to suggest lengths and wording. Then choose how long one appointment lasts: 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or Other… for any multiple of 15 minutes.

The length you pick here is one "hour" your customer books. Customers who want longer sessions can book several in a row — you allow that in step 3.

Step 2 — Working hours​

Start from a ready-made pattern — Weekdays 9–18, Evenings 16–22, Mon–Sat, Every day 10–20 or Weekdays with a lunch break — then adjust the days. The panel on the right shows what your customers will see at checkout and how many appointments fit in a day and a week.

At least one day must be open to continue: a store with no open hours has nothing to sell.

Step 3 — Product​

Decide what the customer buys:

  • Create a new product — enter a name and the price of one hour. Slotiva creates a published, virtual WooCommerce product.
  • Use an existing product — pick one of your products and set its price of one hour. If the product is a draft or private, the wizard tells you it will be published.

Then answer Can a customer book more than one hour at a time?

  • No, one hour — one appointment per order, fixed price.
  • Yes — the price is multiplied by the number of hours. Set At most per order (2 to 10); the table below shows the price for each number of hours.

Step 4 — Checkout check​

Slotiva checks three things every time this step opens:

  • Time zone — a city time zone (for example Europe/Istanbul) is fine. A fixed UTC offset (such as +03:00) is marked Check, because it does not follow daylight saving changes. Change it in Settings → General.
  • Payment method — at least one payment method should be enabled in WooCommerce.
  • Checkout block — the checkout page must use the Checkout block. If it does not, the button Convert the checkout page to blocks opens the page editor: select the Classic Checkout block and choose Transform into blocks.

Items marked Check do not stop you; you can continue or choose I'll do this later.

Step 5 — Done​

A summary of your service, working hours, product and checkout page. Change takes you back to that step (the checkout row has it only when something needs attention). Before your first customer, use Try a booking (opens your product page in a new tab) to book a test appointment yourself: see the time close and the emails arrive.

The setup checklist​

Until the three required items — working hours, checkout page and a bookable product — are done, Slotiva → Appointments shows a checklist above the list. It disappears by itself once setup is complete, or you can hide it.

Running the wizard again​

Go to Slotiva → Settings and click Open the setup wizard. It starts with your current settings and your existing bookable product selected.
